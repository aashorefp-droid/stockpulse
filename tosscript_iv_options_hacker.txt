# TRADE IDEAS when anomaly found:
#   - SPIKE (IV too high): Sell vertical spread or iron condor
#   - DIP (IV too low): Buy calendar spread or long straddle
#   - IV > HV: Sell premium (covered calls, cash-secured puts)
#   - IV < HV: Buy premium (debit spreads, long options)
#   - Mean Reversion: Sell when Z>2, Buy when Z<-2
